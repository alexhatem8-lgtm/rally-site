Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 26, 2026

You can delete your Rally account and its data at any time, from the app or from this page. Rally is operated by Rally Gameday, LLC.

Delete from the app (fastest)

1. Open Rally and go to the Profile tab. 2. Tap the gear icon to open Settings. 3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account". 4. Confirm. Your account is deactivated immediately and scheduled for permanent deletion.

Delete by request (no app needed)

Email support@rallyfindyourcrowd.com from the email address on your Rally account with the subject "Delete my account". We verify the request belongs to you and process it the same way as an in-app deletion. We respond within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, username, photo, bio, favorite teams)
  • Your posts, comments, likes, and photos
  • Your messages leave conversations you were part of
  • Your friend connections and communities memberships
  • Your notification and device tokens

What we keep, and why

  • Purchase and payout records for tickets and paid events are kept as long as tax and payment regulations require. These are financial records tied to completed transactions, not a live profile.
  • Moderation records (reports, enforcement history) are kept where needed to keep the platform safe and to comply with law.
  • Backups age out on a fixed schedule; deleted data leaves backups as they rotate.

Deletion is permanent. A deleted account cannot be restored, and your username becomes available to others after deletion.
