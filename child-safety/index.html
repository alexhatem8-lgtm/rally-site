Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Rally is operated by Rally Gameday, LLC. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for child sexual abuse material (CSAM), in any form, anywhere on our platform.

An adult platform

Rally is for adults. Account creation requires confirming you are 18 or older, and the app is listed for ages 18 and over. Accounts identified as belonging to minors are removed.

Prohibited conduct

The following are prohibited and result in immediate account removal:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing, or requesting CSAM of any kind
  • Sexualizing, grooming, or attempting to contact minors
  • Using Rally to arrange harm to a minor, on or off the platform

How we detect and enforce

  • Every uploaded image is automatically scanned by content moderation systems before it is distributed.
  • Users can report any account, message, post, or event from inside the app. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team.
  • Users can block any account at any time.
  • Violating content is removed and the account is terminated. We preserve and report material as required by law.

Reporting to authorities

We comply with applicable child safety laws. Confirmed or suspected CSAM is reported to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to relevant regional and national authorities, and we cooperate with law enforcement requests.

Contact

Our designated child safety point of contact is support@rallyfindyourcrowd.com. This contact can speak to our CSAE prevention practices and compliance.

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact local law enforcement first. In the United States you can also contact NCMEC at CyberTipline.org or 1-800-843-5678.
