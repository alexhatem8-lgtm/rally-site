Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rally collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects information when you use the Rally mobile application and related services (collectively, "Rally"). Rally is operated by Rally Gameday, LLC ("Rally," "we," "us," or "our"). Contact us at support@rallyfindyourcrowd.com.

1. Who may use Rally

Rally is intended only for adults age 18 and older. We do not knowingly permit anyone under 18 to use Rally. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account or provided information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

2. Information we collect

Account and identity information

  • Email address, login codes, a password hash if you set a password, account identifiers, session records, and account status.
  • Date of birth, which is used for Rally's 18+ eligibility rule and age-related profile or discovery features.
  • If you use Sign in with Apple, the Apple account identifier and email or relay email Apple provides.
  • An optional phone number and verification status if phone verification is offered or used.

Profile and social information

  • Display name, username, optional bio, gender selection, home city, home coordinates, profile photos, social links, favorite teams, fan tags, ratings, trust indicators, and notification preferences.
  • Friendships, blocks, group and community memberships, event attendance, reactions, likes, follows, and other social interactions.

Location and place information

  • With permission, precise or approximate device location while you use location-based features.
  • Home location, gathering locations, addresses, meeting points, and user-drawn boundaries that you choose to submit.

Rally does not request background location and does not provide live location sharing between users.

Content and communications

  • Posts, comments, direct and group messages, reactions, photos, community content, trips and plans, events, tailgates, watch parties, and other material you submit.
  • Reports, report details and snapshots, blocks, moderation decisions, and support communications.
  • Metadata associated with content, such as sender, recipients, channel, timestamps, edits, deletion state, and upload information.

Marketplace and transaction information

  • Ticket listings, quotes, purchases, sales, transaction history, fees, disputes, ratings, buyer transfer email, and transfer-proof screenshots or documents.
  • Stripe account identifiers, payment and refund status, and seller onboarding or payout status. Stripe collects payment-card, identity-verification, and bank-account details through its own interfaces; Rally does not receive or store complete card or bank account numbers.

Device, usage, and diagnostics

  • Push notification token, device platform, operating-system and app information, IP address, device or user-agent information associated with login sessions, notification activity, and security or rate-limit information.
  • Crash reports, errors, stack traces, and related device or diagnostic information through Sentry in non-development builds.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create, authenticate, secure, and administer accounts;
  • enforce age eligibility and account restrictions;
  • provide profiles, social features, chat, events, gatherings, communities, trips, maps, recommendations, and notifications;
  • process marketplace transactions, seller onboarding, transfers, refunds, disputes, fraud controls, and legally required records;
  • operate report, block, moderation, safety, and abuse-prevention systems;
  • diagnose errors, protect the service, maintain availability, and improve performance;
  • respond to support requests and communicate important service changes; and
  • comply with law, enforce our Terms, and protect users, Rally, and others.

Rally does not sell personal information, use it for cross-company advertising tracking, or display third-party behavioral advertising.

4. When information is visible to other users

Depending on the feature and your settings, other users may see profile information, photos, posts, comments, reactions, event or group membership, hosted gatherings, marketplace listings, ratings, and content shared in a conversation or community. Private messages and restricted group content are intended for conversation members, but recipients can save or reshare what they receive. Blocking affects visibility and contact inside Rally but cannot remove copies another person already saved. Rally chat is not end-to-end encrypted; do not use it to send highly sensitive information.

Marketplace counterparties receive information needed to complete a transaction. For example, a seller receives the buyer's ticket-transfer email after payment authorization. Authorized administrators may review reports, associated snapshots, transaction records, and transfer proof for support, safety, fraud, or dispute resolution.

5. Service providers and other disclosures

We disclose information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes in this policy:

  • Cloudflare R2 or another configured S3-compatible provider: photos, uploads, and private marketplace transfer proof.
  • Render: API hosting, databases (including chat messages), logs, caches, and background jobs.
  • Resend: delivery of account login emails.
  • Stripe: payment processing, refunds, seller identity and payout onboarding, fraud prevention, and transaction records.
  • Sentry: crash, error, device, and diagnostic information.
  • AWS Rekognition, when configured: images submitted for automated content screening.
  • Twilio, when configured: optional phone verification and SMS delivery.
  • Expo and Apple Push Notification service: push tokens and notification delivery.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and platform services used at your direction.
  • Apple Maps, Google Maps, OpenStreetMap/Nominatim, and Photon/Komoot: mapping, directions, geocoding, or place-search queries and optional nearby coordinates.

Sports schedule providers supply Rally with event data and ordinarily do not receive Rally account information.

We may also disclose information when required by valid legal process; to investigate fraud, abuse, threats, or illegal content; to protect rights and safety; or as part of a business reorganization, financing, acquisition, or transfer, subject to appropriate protections and notice where required.

6. Retention and account deletion

We retain information while an account is active and as reasonably needed for the uses described above. Different records have different retention periods.

You can request account deletion from Settings → Delete Account. The current deletion process marks the account deleted, blocks subsequent API access, revokes API sessions, removes notification devices and public profile fields, and de-identifies or hides certain records in Rally's database. If an account participated in a marketplace transaction involving money during the previous 30 days, some of that database cleanup may be delayed for up to 30 additional days to handle chargebacks, disputes, fraud, and transaction integrity.

When the deletion completes, uploaded photos and files are removed from storage and any Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked. Messages you sent in group or event chats may remain visible to the other members, attributed to a deleted-account placeholder rather than your name, because removing them would alter conversations other people took part in; you can delete individual messages before deleting your account.

Transaction, refund, payout, tax, safety-report, fraud-prevention, audit, and legal records may be retained longer when reasonably necessary or required by law. Deleted data may remain temporarily in backups and service-provider systems until their normal deletion cycles complete. Data retained for these limited purposes is not used to keep the deleted account active.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Profile: edit or remove available profile fields in Rally.
  • Location, photos, and notifications: change permissions in device settings. You can use manual place search where the feature provides it.
  • Communications: update notification preferences and mute conversations in Rally.
  • Block and report: block users and report supported content using in-app tools.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: use account settings or contact us. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to obtain a copy of information, correct it, object to or restrict certain processing, or appeal a denied privacy request.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a privacy request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

8. Security

We use safeguards designed to protect information, including encrypted network connections, access controls, password hashing, scoped service credentials, session revocation, audit logging, and access restrictions designed to limit transfer-proof files to authorized requests.No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access or loss will never occur.

9. United States processing

Rally is currently offered in the United States. Information may be processed and stored in the United States by Rally and its service providers. If availability expands, we will update this policy and implement any additional required safeguards.

10. Promotions & referrals

Rally may run occasional promotions, giveaways, or referral programs. If you participate, we use your account email and referral activity to administer entries, select winners, and prevent fraud, as described in each promotion's official rules. Referral codes identify the friend who invited you.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Rally changes. We will post the revised version with a new effective date and provide additional notice of material changes when appropriate.

12. Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to support@rallyfindyourcrowd.com.
